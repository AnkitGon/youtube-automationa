Features

Everything automated

From topic research to published video — every step runs without you.

🤖
AI Script Generation
LLM writes a full video script based on your niche, what's performing on your channel, and the strategy the agent derived from analytics.
🎙️
Natural TTS Voice
Edge TTS synthesizes natural-sounding narration in any language. No API cost, no robotic voice. Sounds like a real human presenter.
🎬
Automatic Video Editing
Downloads relevant stock clips from Pexels, syncs them to the narration with FFmpeg, adds background music. Full montage, zero effort.
🖼️
AI Thumbnail
Generates a custom thumbnail image via Pollinations.ai or HuggingFace FLUX, then overlays the video title with Pillow. Click-optimized.
📊
Analytics-Driven
Reads your YouTube channel performance before every video. Adapts topic selection, pacing, and tone based on what's actually working.
⚡
Crash Recovery
Pipeline checkpoints save progress after each stage. If something fails mid-render, the agent resumes from the last completed step — not from scratch.
